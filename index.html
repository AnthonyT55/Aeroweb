Frequently asked

The questions we get before every signed engagement.

What's the minimum engagement? +
The Pilot is a flat $1,500 with 7-day delivery — that's the entry. Operating System engagements are custom-scoped and custom-priced, by Fit Call only. If you're not sure which fits you, apply for the call and we'll tell you honestly.
Do I actually own the work? +
Yes. Every account — Firebase, Stripe, Twilio, n8n, your domain — is set up under your ownership on Day 1, not at handoff. If you fire us tomorrow, nothing breaks. That's the point.
Can you really ship in 21 days? +
Yes. APM went from discovery call to live operating system in 21 days — site, CRM, automation, AI reports. Scope locks before Day 1. We don't miss dates. Read the full APM build breakdown →
What's the retainer actually for? +
SaaS rent buys you access. Our retainer buys you stewardship. Infrastructure oversight, dev support, feature additions, quarterly optimization — handled by the team that built the system, not a ticket queue. It replaces $400–$800/mo of HubSpot, Jobber, and Airtable subscriptions. You stop renting. We hold the stack.
Why are your prices higher than a Squarespace shop? +
Because we're not building the same thing. A template shop sells you a layout. We build you the system underneath it — CRM, admin panel, automation, owned infrastructure. Our Operating System engagement typically replaces $400–$800/month in SaaS subscriptions. Most clients net-positive within 18 months.
What if I'm not sure which tier is right? +
Apply for a Fit Call. We'll look at your current stack, your revenue, and the two or three biggest bottlenecks you're dealing with — and tell you, honestly, which tier fits. If nothing fits, we'll tell you who to call instead.
